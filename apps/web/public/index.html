What Momo can use

Momo uses websites in a browser window of its own. It starts with a copy of your Chrome or Edge sign-ins and saved passwords, so it's already logged in where you are; your own browser isn't touched.

Momo does what you text and tells you what it did; it doesn't stop to ask. This is the one exception: a single purchase above this amount waits for your OK. Clear the box for no limit.
