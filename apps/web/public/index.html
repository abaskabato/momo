Momo is a personal assistant that gets your errands done.

There is nothing new to learn: you text it. It uses websites and your computer the same way you do, and it asks before it books, buys, sends or deletes anything.

It can book a table for Friday, order groceries, send flowers to a friend, renew a prescription, or find a court for the weekend.

It lives on your own computer, so what it knows stays with you. Getting it takes two minutes: download it, open it, and tap a link to start texting.

It lives on your own computer, and this page sets it up. Anything you type here goes only to the company that issued it, never to this site.

2Make your Telegram bot

In Telegram, open @BotFather, send /newbot, pick any name, and paste the token it gives you here.

3What Momo can use

Momo uses websites in a browser of its own, so it doesn't touch yours.

4Get Momo

One small file. Open it, and it installs Momo and starts it in the background, now and whenever this computer starts. Nothing else is installed system-wide.

When it says it's running, tap your bot and say hi. Leave the computer on: Momo works while it's awake.
